## ✅ What the examiner expects in a Testing Log (plain English)

A good testing log shows:

*   Different **types of tests** (normal, boundary, erroneous, absent)
*   Testing across **inputs, processes, database actions**
*   Evidence that testing **led to fixes**
*   **Regression testing** after fixes

They do **not** expect:

*   Automated testing
*   Hundreds of tests
*   Every possible edge case

Quality > quantity.
